Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Voltline Electrical collects when you contact us, and how we use and protect it.

We take your privacy seriously and only collect the information we need to respond to your enquiry and carry out work you ask us to do.

Who we are

Voltline Electrical is the data controller for the information you provide. You can reach us on 01234 567 890 or by email at hello@example.co.uk.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or call us, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone, email, postcode)
  • Details of the service or problem you are enquiring about
  • Any message you choose to send us

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • Carry out and certify any work you ask us to do
  • Contact you about your enquiry or booking

Our lawful basis is your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and running our business. We do not sell your information, and we never share it with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to deal with your request and to meet our legal and insurance obligations, then delete it.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To do so, contact us using the details above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website uses only the cookies needed to make it work and, where enabled, an embedded Google map. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
